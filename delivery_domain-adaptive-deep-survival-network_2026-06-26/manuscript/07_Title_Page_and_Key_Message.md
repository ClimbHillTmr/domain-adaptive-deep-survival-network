## Running Title
Domain-adaptive survival prediction for IDH

## Key Message
Substantial cross-center hemodynamic shift exists between Shenyi and Fuding. A domain-adaptive survival framework improves external discrimination over a zero-shot baseline, while real case-level calibration and decision-curve analyses show potential clinical utility despite residual underestimation of absolute risk.
