## Working Title Options
1. Transport-Aware Survival Prediction of Intradialytic Hypotension Across Two Hemodialysis Centers
2. Target-Center Updated Deep Survival Modeling for Intradialytic Hypotension Timing Under Cross-center Hemodynamic Shift
3. Patient-level Held-out Evaluation of a Locally Updated Survival Model for Intradialytic Hypotension

## Running Title
Transport-aware survival prediction for IDH

## Key Message
Substantial cross-center hemodynamic shift exists between Shenyi and Fuding. A target-center updated survival framework is designed to improve held-out target-center discrimination over a source-only zero-shot baseline, while real case-level calibration and decision-curve analyses should be used to quantify clinical utility and residual underestimation of absolute risk.

## Keywords
Hemodialysis; intradialytic hypotension; target-center updating; survival analysis; external validation; calibration; decision curve analysis
