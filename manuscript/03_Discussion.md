# Discussion

## Interpretation Pending Unified Rerun

Historical model results were removed because they combined incompatible splits, checkpoints, calibration exports, and comparison files. The current study should therefore be described as a two-centre retrospective evaluation of target-centre labeled updating, not as a completed demonstration of architectural superiority or clinical utility.

## Planned Interpretation

The regenerated analysis will distinguish source-only transport, local Cox updating, staged target-centre updating, linear-tokenizer control, and no-adversarial-alignment control. Pre-dialysis vital signs and prior-only historical summaries will be described as predictive feature blocks only if their prespecified, paired analyses support that conclusion. KAN tokenization and adversarial alignment will not be called beneficial unless their paired multi-seed differences exclude no effect.

## Clinical Boundary

Even if updated models retain external discrimination, retrospective prediction evidence cannot establish intervention benefit. Fixed-horizon calibration and decision analyses will be evaluated only among cases with known horizon status, and any deployment claim will remain limited to prospective silent-mode validation after centre-specific recalibration.

## Limitations

The study remains retrospective and limited to two centres. Target-centre labels are used during updating and model selection, so the approach is local labeled updating rather than unsupervised domain adaptation. Institutional review board, waiver, and data-governance statements remain required before submission.
