## Working Title Options
1. Transport-Aware Survival Prediction of Intradialytic Hypotension Across Two Hemodialysis Centers

3. Patient-level Held-out Evaluation of a Locally Updated Survival Model for Intradialytic Hypotension

## Running Title
Transport-aware survival prediction for IDH

## Key Message
A domain-adaptive deep survival network (CDAN-GSN) trained on 216,604 sessions from one hemodialysis centre and locally updated with ~172 labelled sessions from a second centre achieves a C-index of 0.841 (95% CI 0.831–0.852) on a patient-held-out external test set, significantly outperforming both a zero-shot baseline (C-index 0.832, Δ +0.009) and a locally refitted CoxPH model (C-index 0.838, p = 0.004 by bootstrap permutation). Calibration analysis reveals systematic underestimation of absolute event probability (observed 14.5% vs predicted 10.9% at 60 min; observed 24.7% vs predicted 19.3% at 120 min), indicating that centre-specific recalibration is required before clinical deployment.
